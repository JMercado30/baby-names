The Story of Javier

Over a Century of Baby Name Trends in the United States

Overview

The name Javier (the Spanish equivalent of Xavier, derived from the Basque name Xabier) has a rich history in the United States. Over the past century, more than 108,000 boys have been registered with the name in Social Security records.

Historical Insights

  • The Early Years (1917–1950s): Javier was relatively rare in official U.S. records, averaging under 100 births annually.
  • The Rapid Rise (1960s–1990s): Reflecting demographic growth and greater cultural representation, the name saw steady growth, crossing 1,000 births per year by the mid-1970s and 2,000 by 1990.
  • The Peak (2001): The name reached its highest popularity in 2001 with 2,637 births.
  • Recent Decades (2010s–2025): Popularity has stabilized around 1,200 to 1,500 births annually, remaining an enduring favorite.
